# 📱 RAAS Mobile Guide

## Using RAAS on iPhone

RAAS is fully optimized for mobile devices and can be installed on your iPhone home screen like a native app!

---

## 🎯 Quick Install (iPhone/iPad)

### Step 1: Open RAAS in Safari
1. Open **Safari** on your iPhone or iPad
2. Navigate to your published RAAS URL (e.g., `your-app.replit.app`)

### Step 2: Add to Home Screen
1. Tap the **Share button** (square with arrow pointing up) at the bottom of Safari
2. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
3. Edit the name if you want (default: "RAAS")
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right

### Step 3: Launch RAAS
1. Find the RAAS icon on your iPhone home screen (⚡ lightning bolt icon)
2. Tap to open - it launches fullscreen like a native app!

## 📋 Creating Reminders on Mobile

### **Sidebar Forms**
On mobile, the sidebar becomes a collapsible panel:
1. Tap the **">"** button in top-left to open sidebar
2. Fill in reminder details
3. Tap **"Add Todo"** to create

### **Editing Reminders**
1. Tap the **✏️ (edit)** button on any reminder
2. Modify details in the sidebar form
3. Tap **"Save Changes"**

### **Quick Actions**
- **✓ (checkmark)**: Mark as complete
- **✏️ (pencil)**: Edit reminder
- **🗑️ (trash)**: Delete reminder

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### **Can't Find "Add to Home Screen"**
- Make sure you're using Safari (not Chrome/Firefox)
- Scroll down in the Share menu
- Try refreshing the page first

### **App Won't Open After Installing**
- Delete the app icon
- Reinstall by following steps above
- Make sure you have stable internet

### **Forms Are Zooming In**
- This is fixed in the latest version
- If still happening, update RAAS or refresh

### **Sidebar Not Showing**
- Tap the **">"** icon in top-left corner
- On small screens, sidebar becomes collapsible
